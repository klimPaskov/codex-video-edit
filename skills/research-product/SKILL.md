# Research product and platform

## Use when

A current capability, competitor pattern, operating-system behavior, or external API may have changed.

## Procedure

1. Define the exact unknown.
2. Use public browser research in the isolated environment.
3. Prefer official and primary sources for technical behavior.
4. Compare publication date and event date.
5. Record source, date, finding, uncertainty, and affected decision.
6. Separate observed competitor behavior from desired product behavior.
7. Never copy protected text, branding, icons, images, or exact layout.
8. Update `research/RESEARCH.md` and any affected ADR.

## Required areas

- Electron capture and security
- Playwright Electron testing
- FFmpeg and local transcription
- official Codex app-server and ChatGPT login
- Borumi and comparable creator workflows

## Stop condition

When primary documentation is missing or conflicting, record the uncertainty and design a test instead of inventing a contract.
